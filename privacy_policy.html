Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 5, 2026

1. Introduction

VideoPublisher ("the Application") is a personal desktop application designed for local use. This privacy policy explains how the application handles your data.

2. Information We Collect

The application collects and stores locally:

  • Authentication Tokens: OAuth tokens for TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube
  • User Preferences: Application settings and configurations
  • Local File Paths: Paths to videos you choose to upload

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate with social media platforms on your behalf
  • Upload and publish your video content
  • Maintain your application preferences

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. The application:

  • Does NOT transmit your data to external servers (except to the social media platforms you authorize)
  • Does NOT share your information with third parties
  • Does NOT collect analytics or telemetry
  • Stores authentication credentials in encrypted format in your device's secure storage

5. Third-Party Services

The application connects to:

  • TikTok: Subject to TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Instagram/Facebook: Subject to Meta Privacy Policy
  • YouTube: Subject to Google Privacy Policy

When you authorize the application to access these services, their respective privacy policies apply to the data they collect and process.

6. Data Security

We implement security measures including:

  • Encrypted storage of authentication credentials
  • OAuth 2.0 industry-standard authentication
  • No storage of passwords
  • Local-only data storage

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete all locally stored data at any time
  • Revoke application access to your social media accounts
  • Request information about stored data

8. Data Retention

Data is retained locally until you:

  • Manually delete it through the application
  • Uninstall the application
  • Revoke authentication tokens

9. Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last Updated" date.
